# Multi-Enemy Future Expansion Gate

Status: `Deferred — No Pilot 3 Pair Qualified For Ordinary Rollout`

The shipped engine supports explicit one- or two-enemy encounters, while
ordinary random generation remains singleton. No post-foundation Pilot 3 pair
met every aggregate gate, so the default-on rollout switch has no qualified
catalog entry. Do not use local encounter multipliers or global numeric tuning
to force a rollout.

Before registering a floor-5 candidate, approve:

1. representative second-promotion lineages or a broader stratified class
   matrix;
2. floor-5 benchmark level and equipment assumptions;
3. enemy-authored area-action behavior for the single active player-side slot;
   and
4. whether endgame enemies shared with floor 6 are eligible.

Any future ordinary pair must pass the approved aggregate bands without invalid
intents or max-turn loops: 55–75% player wins, 1.25–2.0 times the harder
singleton actor turns, and 20–60% median winning-player HP. Rosters larger than
two require a separate action-model and layout contract.

The current runtime contract is owned by
[`MULTI_ENEMY_COMBAT_DESIGN.md`](MULTI_ENEMY_COMBAT_DESIGN.md). The completed
Pilot 3 candidates, matrices, and manual evidence are browsable history in
[`history/MULTI_ENEMY_PILOT_3_PLAN.md`](history/MULTI_ENEMY_PILOT_3_PLAN.md).
