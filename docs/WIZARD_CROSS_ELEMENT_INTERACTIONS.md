# Wizard Cross-Element Interactions

This is a developer-only reference. These interactions are deliberately omitted
from player-facing ability descriptions so players can discover them in combat.

| Wizard passive | Hidden interaction | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inferno | Fire Whirl | When Ejection Gale triggers, it becomes a Fire Whirl that deals major Fire and Wind damage. A surviving target is still ejected. |
| Subzero | Earth | An Unsteady Ground trigger can shatter an enemy frozen by Subzero. |
| Electrical Burns | Capital Punishment | An enemy drowning from Aspirate can be killed instantly by Electric magic. |
| Divine Wind | Raijin | Electric magic deals double damage to an enemy left prone by Divine Wind. |
| Unrelenting Waves | Flash Frozen | A Snowpiercer trigger can freeze a target affected by Unrelenting Waves and leave it brittle. |
| Aftershock | Pyroclast | A Combustion trigger can turn ground affected by Aftershock into molten lava, dealing intense burning damage over several turns. |

The visible standalone effects remain unchanged: Inferno spreads burns,
Subzero freezes, Electrical Burns ignites on Electric critical hits, Divine
Wind manipulates flight and applies prone, Unrelenting Waves recurs, and
Aftershock repeatedly damages enemies through the ground.
